How to read these tables

The selected Gameweek shows results played before the next regular round begins. Postponed fixtures are included when they were actually played, not retroactively. P = played, W = wins, D = draws, L = losses, GF = goals for, GA = goals against, GD = goal difference, Pts = points. Optional columns include YC = yellow cards, RC = red cards, Sh = shots, SOT = shots on target, Cor = corners, F = fouls committed.

Enable Vs. final position to see how many places the club will rise or fall by the end of the season: dark green up = 10+ places gained, medium green up = 4–9 gained, light green up = 1–3 gained, orange down = 1–3 lost, light red down = 4–9 lost, dark red down = 10+ lost, and equals = unchanged. Click a club to keep it highlighted across seasons. Enable “Connect across seasons” to draw arrows only in the gaps between neighboring tables; their colors compare the club’s position from the older season on the right to the newer season on the left at the selected Gameweek. For the current season, the final rank doesn't exist yet, so no comparison appears. Historical ranking follows points, goal difference and goals scored, with team-name fallback for unresolved ties; rare league tiebreakers and points deductions aren't yet modeled.
